Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bachelor Book ("we", "us", "the app", Android package app.bachelorbook) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Bachelor Book mobile application and related backend services (together, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email address, display name, profile photo URL, and a unique Google/Firebase account identifier. We never receive or store your Google password.

Mess and bookkeeping data. Inside a mess you create or join, we store the shared records you and your messmates enter: daily meal status (breakfast, lunch, dinner), scheduled meal opt-outs, guest meal counts, meal cutoff times, bazaar/repair/other expenses, approval statuses, utility bills, seat rent amounts, invite codes, audit-log entries, and monthly settlement snapshots.

Device and notification data. If you grant notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token and a basic platform label (Android/iOS) so we can deliver mess updates (such as expense approvals or meal changes). You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Camera permission (QR scanner). When you choose to join a mess by scanning a roommate's QR code, the app requests camera access (CAMERA). Camera frames are processed strictly on-device to decode the 8-character invite code. We never capture, store, or transmit photos, videos, or camera feeds.

Advertising data. When ads are enabled in the free version of the app, Google AdMob may collect device-level identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate IP-based location, and ad-interaction diagnostics to serve ads, cap frequency, measure performance, and prevent fraud. See Section 9 for details and your controls.

Technical and diagnostic logs. Our backend servers, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and AdMob process standard technical metadata (timestamps, IP addresses, HTTP status codes, and crash or request diagnostics) to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use your information

  • To authenticate your account and manage your mess memberships.
  • To display shared meals, expenses, utilities, audit logs, and monthly calculations to active members of your mess.
  • To send push notifications about mess activity when you have granted permission.
  • To show and measure advertising through Google AdMob so the app can remain free.
  • To maintain service reliability, enforce access controls, and prevent abuse.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your mess records (meals, bazaar expenses, rent, or calculations) with advertisers.
  • We do not upload or store images or video from your camera.
  • We do not read your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, or precise GPS location.

4. Who else can see your data

Other active members of the same mess can see your display name, seat rent, and the mess records you participate in. Shared visibility is the core purpose of Bachelor Book so every roommate sees the same numbers.

We use the following third-party service providers (sub-processors) to run the Service:

  • Google Firebase — Google Sign-In authentication and Firebase Cloud Messaging push notifications.
  • Microsoft Azure — hosts the Bachelor Book backend API.
  • Aiven — provides the managed PostgreSQL database storing application records.
  • Google AdMob — serves and measures in-app advertising.

Strict mess access control. A mess's records are accessible only to active members of that mess. Requests from non-members — or former members who left or were removed — are rejected at the API layer.

5. Data retention

We retain your account profile and mess records while your account remains active. FCM device tokens are removed when you sign out, delete your account, or when tokens expire.

When you leave a shared mess, your membership is end-dated and your access ends immediately, while historical meal and expense counts up to your departure date remain part of the mess's financial record for the remaining members.

AdMob advertising identifiers and analytics follow the retention periods described in Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Account deletion and your rights

Instant in-app account deletion: You can permanently delete your account directly inside Bachelor Book at any time — go to Mess tab → Danger Zone → Delete Account (or tap Delete Account on the Welcome screen if you are not in a mess). See our Account Deletion Guide for full details or to submit a deletion request without the app installed.

When you delete your account:

  • Deleted immediately: Your name, email address, profile photo URL, sign-in identifier, and all FCM push notification tokens are permanently erased. Any solo mess where you were the only member is permanently deleted.
  • De-identified for roommate accuracy: In shared messes with other roommates, your membership is closed (another member is automatically promoted if you were the sole manager) and your past numerical meal/expense totals remain de-identified as "Deleted Member" so your roommates' historical calculations do not break.

You can also at any time:

  • Leave any mess from inside the app (Mess tab → Danger Zone → Leave Mess).
  • Revoke notification or camera permissions in your Android system settings.
  • Reset or delete your Android Advertising ID (Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads).
  • Opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com.
  • Request data export or deletion by emailing bachelorbookapp@gmail.com from your sign-in email address (we respond within 30 days).

7. Children's privacy

Bachelor Book is intended for adults and college/university flatmates and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable digital consent age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at bachelorbookapp@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

8. Security

All communication between the mobile app and our backend uses encrypted HTTPS (TLS). API requests are authenticated with short-lived Google/Firebase ID tokens and authorized per mess membership on the server. While we follow industry-standard safeguards, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% immune to risk.

9. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Bachelor Book displays ads provided by Google AdMob to support server and hosting costs. Google may use your Android Advertising ID, device signals, and IP address to serve and measure ads as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use our services.

You can control ad personalization at any time by:

  • Deleting or resetting your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Managing your preferences at adssettings.google.com.

Your private mess records (meals, bazaar lists, utilities, rent) are never shared with Google AdMob.

10. International data processing

Your data is processed by the cloud sub-processors listed in Section 4 on managed infrastructure that may be located outside your country of residence. Each provider documents its security posture and compliance certifications on the pages linked in Section 4.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. For material changes, we will provide notice inside the app.

12. Contact

If you have questions, privacy concerns, or data requests, email us at bachelorbookapp@gmail.com.
