Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-01

This Privacy Policy describes how Bachelor Book ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Bachelor Book mobile application and related services (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email, display name, profile photo URL, and a stable Firebase user ID. We do not receive your Google password.

Mess data. Inside a mess you create or join, we store the records you and other members choose to add: meal selections, meal opt-outs, guest meals, bazaar and repair expenses, utilities, rent amounts, invite codes, manager votes, audit-log entries, and settlement calculations.

Device and push data. If you grant notification permission, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token so the app can send you mess updates. We store a short device-info string (model, OS) sent at registration. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Advertising data. When ads are shown in the app, Google AdMob may collect or receive device-level identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), coarse location derived from your IP address, and information about which ads you see and interact with. This data is used by Google to select and serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. See Google's documentation linked in Section 9 for details.

Usage data. Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Messaging, our backend, and AdMob log standard technical metadata (timestamps, request status codes) to keep the Service running and secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and let you join messes.
  • To show your mess's shared records to other active members of the same mess.
  • To send push notifications about mess activity (when you have granted permission).
  • To show advertising through Google AdMob and measure how ads perform.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, and to keep the Service operational.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your mess records with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites outside what AdMob does on Google's behalf as described above.

4. Who else can see your data

Other active members of the same mess can see the mess records you add. This is the point of the app — full transparency inside a mess.

We use the following sub-processors to operate the Service:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and push notifications.
  • Microsoft Azure — runs the application logic (the backend) on cloud servers.
  • Aiven — provides the managed database where mess records are stored.
  • Google AdMob — serves and measures advertising shown in the app.

Each sub-processor acts on our behalf and is bound by its own privacy and security terms. The links above lead to each provider's compliance page, where the regions, retention, and security posture of their infrastructure are documented. We do not transfer your data to any other third party.

Access control. A mess's records are visible only to that mess's currently active members. The backend enforces this at the API layer: requests from users who are not active members of the mess are rejected, and users who have left or been removed cannot see any of that mess's data — including past activity — unless and until they are re-added with a fresh invite.

5. Data retention

Account data and mess records are kept for as long as your account is active. When a mess is closed by its members, the records are retained for a period after closure in case of dispute, then deleted from active systems. Audit-log entries may be retained longer in aggregated form. FCM device tokens are deleted when you sign out or revoke notification permission. AdMob advertising identifiers follow the retention practices described in Google's privacy policy.

When you leave a mess, your membership row is end-dated but the mess's own records (meals, expenses, members, audit log) are not deleted — the mess continues to exist and is owned by its remaining members. You lose access to those records as soon as your membership ends (see Section 6).

6. Your rights

You can at any time:

  • View the data associated with your account inside the app.
  • Leave a mess. Leaving immediately removes your access to that mess: the app stops showing it, the backend stops returning any of its data to you, and you will not receive further push notifications about it. To rejoin later, you need a fresh invite from a current member — your old membership is treated as closed and does not give you any read-only access to past records.
  • Sign out and request deletion of your account by contacting us.
  • Reset or delete your Android advertising ID from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

To request export or deletion of your account and the data associated with it, email bachelorbookapp@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We respond within 30 days.

7. Children and families

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. AdMob is configured to treat all ad requests as coming from users above the age threshold required by Google. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

8. Security

We use HTTPS for all traffic between the app and the backend, Firebase short-lived ID tokens for authentication, and access checks at the API layer so users only see records for messes they are active members of. No method of transmission over the internet, however, is 100% secure.

9. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows advertising served by Google AdMob. By using the app you agree that Google may use advertising identifiers and related data to select and serve ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob privacy and messaging documentation.

Default ad personalization. In regions where we are not required by law to collect consent, the app serves non-personalized ads by default. Non-personalized ads use contextual information (such as the app screen you are viewing) but do not use your advertising ID to build a profile or show ads based on your activity across other apps.

Your ad choices. You can:

  • Reset or delete your Android advertising ID (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Opt out of ad personalization on the web at adssettings.google.com.
  • Stop seeing ads entirely by switching to a future paid version of the app, if and when we offer one.

We do not use advertising data for any purpose other than showing and measuring ads inside Bachelor Book. Mess records (meals, expenses, members, rent) are never shared with AdMob or any other advertising provider.

10. Where your data is processed

Your data is processed by the sub-processors listed in Section 4 on cloud infrastructure located in regions chosen to be close to our primary user base. Each sub-processor publishes the regions and security certifications of its infrastructure on its own compliance page (linked in Section 4). For most users this means data is processed in the same region they live in; for users in regions we do not currently serve from a nearby datacenter, data may be processed in another region. We do not transfer your data to any party other than the sub-processors listed above.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service evolves. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If the changes are material, we will notify active users through the app.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: bachelorbookapp@gmail.com.
