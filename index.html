How a mess works

1
Create or join a mess.

A mess is your shared flat. The first person signs in and creates it; everyone else joins with a 7-character invite code.

2
Tap meals as you eat them.

Every day, you check off breakfast, lunch and dinner. Going out? One tap to opt out. Guests over? Add them in two taps.

3
Log bazaar and rent.

Whoever shops drops a bazaar entry. The manager approves. Rent is set per member. Utilities are added once and reused.

4
Close the month.

At month-end, the manager closes the meal-month. The settlement is frozen. Everyone sees the same numbers.
