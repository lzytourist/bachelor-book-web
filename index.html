How a mess works

1
Create or join a mess.

Sign in with Google, create your flat's mess with your meal weight rules, and invite roommates via QR code or 8-character invite code.

2
Toggle meals as plans change.

Meals default to ON. Heading out? Turn slots off before the cutoff or bulk-schedule dates on the calendar. Guests over? Add them in two taps.

3
Log bazaar, utilities & rent.

Whoever does bazaar logs the bill for manager approval. Rent and utility bills are configured once and factored into every member's balance.

4
Close the month with zero guesswork.

At month-end, the manager closes the meal-month. The final settlement is archived in Past Months so everyone can verify what they give or receive.
