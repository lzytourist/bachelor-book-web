Delete Your Bachelor Book Account

You can permanently delete your account and personal data instantly inside the Bachelor Book Android app, or request deletion by email if you have already uninstalled the app.

Option 1: Delete instantly inside the app (Recommended)

  1. Open the Bachelor Book app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to the Mess tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section and tap Delete Account. (If you are not currently in any mess, tap Delete Account at the bottom of the Welcome screen.)
  4. Review the summary in the confirmation dialog and tap Delete Permanently.

Your personal data is erased immediately, you are signed out, and all push notification tokens are revoked.

Option 2: Request deletion by email (Without the app)

If you have uninstalled Bachelor Book or can no longer access the app, send an email from the Google address you used to sign in:

Email bachelorbookapp@gmail.com

We verify ownership via your sign-in email address and process email deletion requests within 7 business days (and no later than 30 days).

What happens to your data

Permanently deleted immediately

  • Display name and email address
  • Profile photo URL and Google/Firebase account link
  • All push notification (FCM) device tokens
  • Any solo mess where you were the only member
  • Your access to all shared messes

Kept de-identified (shared messes only)

  • Historical meal counts and bazaar expense figures in shared messes with other roommates are kept de-identified as "Deleted Member" so your former roommates' monthly bills and past snapshots stay accurate.
  • If you were the sole manager of a shared mess, another active member is automatically promoted to manager before your account is removed.

Need help?

For any questions about your account or data, read our Privacy Policy or contact bachelorbookapp@gmail.com.
